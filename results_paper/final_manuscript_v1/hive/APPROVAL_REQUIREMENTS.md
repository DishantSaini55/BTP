# Institutional approval requirements checklist

- Institutional ethics committee/IRB review and written approval before recruitment.
- Dataset-owner approval for use of any image/video-derived stimulus and confirmation of license/redistribution terms.
- Privacy assessment and approved anonymization for faces, plates and sensitive locations.
- Final protocol, inclusion/exclusion rules, target sample size and analysis plan preregistered.
- Participant information sheet and consent form approved, translated where needed, and accessible.
- Compensation, withdrawal, adverse-event and researcher contact procedures approved.
- Secure response storage, access controls, retention/deletion timetable and de-identification plan.
- Accessibility and color-vision accommodations for all visual displays.
- No claim of model safety, causal explanation, or deployment utility from this behavioral exercise.

All items remain prospective; no approval or participant activity is represented as complete.
