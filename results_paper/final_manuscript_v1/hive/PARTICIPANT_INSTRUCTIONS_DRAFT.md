# Participant instructions draft (not approved; do not deploy)

Thank you for considering this study. You will see short displays of a pedestrian's recent observed path and one or more computer-generated future paths. The paths are shown with neutral labels. They are predictions, not advice, and they may be wrong.

For each display:

1. Review the observed path and the forecast candidates.
2. Choose the forecast that you think most closely matches the pedestrian's later path, or choose **cannot tell** if the display is unclear.
3. Rate how confident you are in your choice using the scale shown.
4. Do not try to infer the computer model or a pedestrian's intention. We are measuring judgments about the displayed paths.
5. You may pause, skip a question, or stop participating at any time without penalty.

The study team will explain how the later path is used for scoring only after your response is recorded, if that reveal is approved in the final protocol. Please do not discuss answers with other participants during the study.

This is a draft only. Trial count, response controls, accessibility options, disclosures, timing and all required contact/withdrawal language must be finalized with institutional ethics approval before recruitment. No study is currently running.
