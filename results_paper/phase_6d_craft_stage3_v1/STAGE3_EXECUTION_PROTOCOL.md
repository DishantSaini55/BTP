**Exploratory development-only adaptation.** The supervisor-provided CRAFT paper and the prior original-paper analysis in `results_paper/phase_6d_craft_addendum_v1/CRAFT_ORIGINAL_PAPER_ANALYSIS.md` govern the recursive NMF and Sobol concepts. This is not a faithful reproduction of CRAFT's image-crop pipeline or implicit-differentiation attribution maps.

## Frozen inputs and boundaries

Use AO-3 seed 20260928, the saved Stage 1 ReLU FFN activations and the canonical Stage 2B rank-4 bank (`SELECTED_FULL_TRAINING_BANK.npz`, first seed 20260928). Verify the checkpoint and activation/split hashes before analysis. Do not recapture final-layer activations, tune the bank, access official-test data, or modify/train the model. The only permitted forward passes beyond the saved final-layer activations are: (a) minimum training-only windows needed to capture the immediately earlier encoder FFN ReLU outputs for recursive concepts; (b) the specified GP7 development interventions.

All work is one Python process, ≤4 CPU threads, and ≤1,800 seconds. Use an exclusive lock, atomic stage markers/output writes, fixed seeds, an execution log, and a wall-clock guard. Stop at the budget. No `paper/` edits, downloads, commit, push, or subsequent phase.

## Recursive factors

For each of four parent concepts, rank all 14,820 training `(window, observed-token)` coefficients by descending coefficient, ties by saved training row then temporal index. Select exactly the first `ceil(0.10 × 14,820) = 1,482` tokens. Preserve window ID, video, track, token index, parent coefficient, and aligned earlier-layer activation. Capture only the unique selected windows, using encoder layer index 1's post-ReLU FFN output (immediately earlier than parent layer index 2). For each parent separately, fit rank-2 NMF to those aligned earlier-layer token vectors with `nndsvdar`, seeds 20260928 and 20260929, maximum 200 iterations, tolerance 0.001. Report fit relative Frobenius error, warnings, iterations, coefficient sparsity, and matched cosine stability (unit-normalized component vectors with Hungarian assignment). A child bank is numerically supported only if both fits converge without warning, fit error ≤0.50, and matched mean cosine ≥0.90. These are project screening thresholds, not CRAFT standards. No semantic label is assigned by inspection.

## Motion associations

The unit is one of the 988 saved training windows; each descriptor uses the 15 raw observed boxes only. Centers are `(x1+x2)/2,(y1+y2)/2`. Descriptors are: (1) net horizontal center displacement `cx[14]-cx[0]` pixels; (2) net vertical displacement `cy[14]-cy[0]` pixels; (3) mean norm of first center differences, pixels per observed step; (4) mean norm of second center differences, pixels per observed-step squared; (5) mean absolute wrapped angle between adjacent nonzero center-velocity vectors, radians; (6) coefficient of variation (standard deviation/mean) of `sqrt(box width × box height)`. Parent score is the temporal mean of its 15 coefficients per window. Compute Spearman rho and two-sided p-values; apply Benjamini-Hochberg over all 24 parent-descriptor tests. A descriptor association is flagged only when `|rho| ≥0.30` and BH `q<0.05`. Report sample count and overlapping-window/pedestrian dependence. Numeric IDs remain the default; a correlation alone is not a semantic concept label.

## Validation sample and Sobol design

Select 32 GP7 windows without looking at predictions, errors, or concept importance: sort validation video IDs, then sample IDs within each video; take 16 windows per video (the frozen cache has two validation videos). Freeze their saved rank-4 coefficients. The four independent Sobol inputs are global amplitude multipliers `lambda_k ~ Uniform(0,1)` for each parent bank: replace the final FFN activation by `sum_k lambda_k U[:,:,k] W[k,:]`. This is a defined independent intervention distribution, not the empirical joint distribution of naturally correlated coefficients. Keep all other inputs, activation pathways, weights, and model state unchanged. Use a scrambled 8-D Sobol design (fixed seed 20611), split into independent 4-D A and B blocks, with nested prefixes of 32, 64, and 128 base rows. Estimate total-order indices with the Jansen estimator `mean((f(A)-f(A_B^k))^2)/(2 Var([f(A),f(B)]))`; report the variance in output units squared and mark negligible-variance outputs/undefined estimates. Validate the implementation before model evaluation on the additive synthetic function `f(x)=x1+2x2+3x3+4x4`, whose total-order indices are `[1,4,9,16]/30`; maximum absolute index error at N=128 must be ≤0.10.

The four frozen scalar functionals, averaged across the 32 selected windows, are: (1) final predicted center-x in pixels; (2) final predicted center-y in pixels; (3) Euclidean distance in pixels between final predicted center and last observed center; and (4) mean squared raw-box-coordinate difference between model prediction and constant-velocity forecast, in pixels squared. For each report indices at all budgets, absolute output variance and the absolute output change from the original model under full reconstruction and zero activation. Interpret indices only as relative sensitivity within this reconstructed activation branch; assess model-level importance using absolute effects and the original/zero controls.

## Insertion/deletion fidelity

For each scalar output, order the four concepts by the N=128 Sobol total-order estimate; also evaluate reverse order and five fixed random orders (seeds 3101, 3102, 3103, 3104, 3105). At each step, activate/deactivate the selected concept amplitude at 0/1 while keeping the other concepts at their current insertion/deletion settings. Report output distance from both the original model and full-reconstruction output. Normalize distance by the absolute full-reconstruction-to-original difference; if that denominator is ≤1e-10, mark normalized distance undefined. Preserve full-reconstruction and zero-activation discrepancies separately. Report curve AUCs over the five steps and random-order mean/SD. Do not claim fidelity validation unless Sobol ordering provides meaningful improvement over reverse/random controls.

## Regression checks, figures, and stop criteria

Run focused helper regression tests in the same process, including the synthetic Sobol check, BH monotonicity, coefficient-to-activation reconstruction, and deterministic top-decile ordering. Save test output. Figures must be generated from saved numeric results only (PNG, SVG, and PDF when available): parent coefficient-by-time profiles, four representative observed training trajectories, recursive hierarchy only for numerically supported factors, motion association heatmap, per-output Sobol bars, absolute effects, insertion/deletion curves, and one deterministic GP7 concept-intervention trajectory overlay. Concept profiles are activation coefficients, not input attribution maps. No image scenes, semantic labels unsupported by the association criteria, human evaluation, or implicit differentiation.

If recursion is numerically unsupported, retain the validated parent bank and document that result. If Sobol convergence or absolute effects are inadequate, report the failure and do not characterize concepts as important. Stop after this experiment; do not expand ranks, budgets, seeds, sample count, or runtime.

## Predeclared convergence and fidelity decision rules

For each scalar output, the Sobol estimate is called numerically converged only if output variance exceeds `1e-12` in its stated units squared, all estimates are finite, and the largest absolute total-order-index change from N=64 to N=128 is ≤0.10. Otherwise report unstable/undefined and do not rank concepts for that output. A fidelity curve is called meaningfully ordered only if Sobol-order insertion AUC of normalized distance from the full-reconstruction output is at least 0.10 lower than both reverse-order and five-seed random-mean AUC, and Sobol-order deletion AUC of that distance is at least 0.10 higher than both controls. This is a project screening rule, not a universal fidelity standard. The denominator for normalized distance is the absolute difference between full-reconstruction and original-output scalar means; when ≤1e-10, mark normalized distances/AUCs undefined and make no normalized fidelity claim.
